# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central guide to OctoAcme’s project management processes, roles, and working practices.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project lifecycle. Teams validate the business need, stakeholders, and success measures during initiation, then make a go/no-go decision before planning. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies and risks.

During execution, teams deliver small increments, track work on a project board, collaborate through regular delivery cadences, test and review changes, and escalate blockers and risks. Risk and stakeholder communication are maintained throughout delivery. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Teams close the loop with retrospectives and tracked improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
